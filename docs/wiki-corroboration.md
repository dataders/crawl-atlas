## Interpretation

The skill collection has favored numeric level announcements. It consequently misses clearly acquired or used abilities whose level is unstated. Missing rows must mean “not captured,” not “the character lacks this ability.” Add source-backed known-with-unknown-level observations and keep equipment-granted abilities distinct from trained base levels.

The wiki [skill mechanics page](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Skills) also warns that higher-level bonuses do not add linearly. This explains why simply summing every listed bonus can disagree with a reported effective skill level. Retain reported totals and provenance instead of manufacturing arithmetic totals.

This document is a targeted crosscheck, not an exhaustive wiki scrape. No wiki-only fact has been added to the public timeline.
